Breaking · Middle East

Two ships sink near Hormuz as the US draws up plans for its biggest bomb

Riyadh airport still shut after Houthi strikes, Taiz cut off from aid, oil hovering above 100 dollars

In briefTwo cargo ships sank near the strait this week. US officials told CNN new plans could use the GBU-43/B, the largest non-nuclear US bomb, on Iranian energy and nuclear sites if combat resumes. It has never been used against Iran.

One was a Singapore-flagged container ship whose 21 crew were all evacuated; the other, an Antigua and Barbuda-flagged vessel, went down weeks after a projectile hit it on 23 September, killing one sailor. The UN is pressing Washington and Tehran to agree a route for fertiliser, about a third of world shipments of which normally pass through Hormuz.

The regional war began on 28 February, when the United States and Israel struck Tehran and more than 100 other sites in Iran. Riyadh's King Khalid airport has been closed since Saturday's Houthi missile strike, the deadliest on Saudi soil since then, which killed at least 13 people.

Lead · Chips

TSMC's record quarter shows the AI boom is real, even as investors flinch

In briefNet profit NT$763 billion (about 24 billion dollars), up 69% on a year. Revenue 46.7 billion dollars, up 41%. The stock still fell about 4%.

The company that makes almost every advanced AI chip, for Nvidia, Apple and AMD alike, extended its run of record profits to six quarters. Its newest 2 nm process jumped from 3% to 8% of wafer revenue in a single quarter, and nodes of 7 nm and below now make up 79% of sales.

So why the drop? Gross margin slipped to 66.4% from 67.7%, and on a day when Seoul crashed over the cost of financing AI, any sign of strain was punished. TSMC guided fourth-quarter revenue to 52.3 to 54.3 billion dollars, another step up. Elsewhere, Google's Gemini 4 Argon went live on 13 October after months of delays, chasing OpenAI's GPT-6.

Lead · Rigging

mGear 5.4 lands with a unified facial rigger and a new Anim Picker

In briefThe free, open-source Maya framework updated on 14 October: unified facial rigging, a redesigned Anim Picker and 3D onion skinning.

The standout tool for riggers is Blendshape Setup Transfer: it moves blendshape setups between character meshes and generates Set Driven Key setups straight from timeline poses. You pose the character and the tool writes the setup, instead of keying values one at a time. Animators get "Blocking Ghosts", semi-transparent copies of the character at each key.

Why it matters to you: mGear is everywhere in studio pipelines, and its new picker sits right next to what you build for AviaRigg. Study its UX before your next picker script.

Tool of the week

Editorial

The price of a missile is paid at the pump, then in Seoul

It is tempting to file sinking ships near Hormuz under "far away". They are not. A war on the world's key oil chokepoint keeps energy dear, dear energy feeds the inflation numbers that came in hot twice this week, and hot inflation raises the cost of the borrowing that builds AI data centres. On Thursday that chain ran all the way to a circuit breaker in Seoul.

The most exposed parts of a portfolio are the ones that live on cheap money: richly valued tech and AI, and crypto. That is not a call to sell. It is an argument for owning the boring, diversified pieces you already planned to own, and not letting one hot sector quietly become a third of everything.

Opinion. The other side: some analysts argue AI earnings are strong enough to outrun higher rates, and that selloffs like Wednesday's are buying chances.

A tip for tonight, and where today's facts came from

Rigging tip · Maya 2026

Stop building offset groups above your controls. Since Maya 2020 every transform has an offsetParentMatrix attribute. Copy the control's world matrix into it (or drive it from a multMatrix or blendMatrix node), then zero the transform: channels read 0 at bind pose with no extra _grp or _offset node.

Python: cmds.setAttr(ctrl + ".offsetParentMatrix", cmds.xform(ctrl, q=True, m=True, ws=True), type="matrix"), then reset translate, rotate and scale. Best on controls parented to the world or an unscaled group.
